# Sarah

<div class="hatnote">Mahm or Mom redirect here.</div>

Also known as Mahm, Mama, or Mom, **Sarah** (she/they) frequently serves as either a cat bed or a bathtub, but her main purpose is providing so very many kisses. The Cinnaboys compete for the coveted napping spot located on her collarbones, while Simon prefers to stand on her leg and make biscuits on the pillow she holds on her lap.

## The Kisses Zone

The Kisses Zone is the space between Mom's face and any cat's forehead. [Toothless](/cats/toothless.md) often took advantage of this space by pressing his forehead directly to her mouth whenever he needed many kisses. Mom, of course, was always happy to oblige.

## Quotes

- "I'm a bathtub..."
    - Fairly self-explanatory. If a cat is bathing themself on your lap, that makes you a bathtub. Similarly, if a cat is cleaning their paws while sitting on you, then you are a sink.
- "Biiiiig stretch!" or "Straaaaatch"
    - Anyone witnessing a Big Stretch is legally obligated to acknowledge said stretch or face strong punitive measures. Big Yawns must be similarly recognized.
