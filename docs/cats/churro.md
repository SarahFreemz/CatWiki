**Churro** is one of the [Cinnaboys](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md), a pair of twin gray tuxedo kittens who recently joined the Fremont household.

## Biography

Churro was found outside with his feline Mama, his [twin](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) brother [Horchata](/cats/horchata.md), and two other kittens. [Dad](/people/jon.md)'s coworker took them in and fostered them until the Twins were old enough to move into the Fremont household. He took quickly to snuggling [Mom](/people/sarah.md), his preferred spot being as high up on her collarbones as he can get. He spends much of his day napping on Mom, completely Boneless, even beginning to drool when he is completely relaxed.

## Appearance & Style

Churro is a beautiful solid gray color, except for his feet and chest, which are white. He has perfect little ankle socks in the front (his business socks) and fluffy crew socks in the back (his party socks) to match his [brother](/cats/horchata.md). His white chest looks like he's wearing a dress shirt under a little jacket. His nose is black and frequently wet.

## Anatomy

Churro is completely Boneless. The location of his bones is currently unknown, although it is suspected that [Horchata](/cats/horchata.md) has hidden them away somewhere for safekeeping. His anatomy, especially while sleeping on Mom, can be described most accurately as a cat-shaped sack of pudding. He also appears to have traits indicating hybridism with several other species, detailed below:

### Bat

Churro has Big Ole Bat Ears, another trait differentiating him from from his brother.

### Sugar Glider/Flying Squirrel

Churro has extra flaps of skin at his armpits, leading researchers to believe he may have some ancestral ties to either flying squirrels or sugar gliders. They appear to be vestigial, as he still has not mastered the art of flight or gliding (or even jumping, for that matter).

## Signature Moves
<div class="hatnote">For a full list of moves/poses, see <a href="/#/additional-observations/moves-and-poses">Moves and Poses</a>.</div>


### BONELESS

When Churro flops, whether on [Mom](/people/sarah.md), [Dad](/people/jon.md), or the [floor](#flop-n-plop), he goes completely Boneless. Researchers have found no sign of his bones, leading them to wonder whether his brother hid them away or if he even had any installed at the [Kitten Factory](/places/kitten-factory.md).

### [Scrambledy Bamble](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=scrambledy-bamble)

<figure class="wiki-thumb right small">

  <img src="/assets/images/IMG_20260718_193501638_HDR.jpg" alt="a blurry photo of a small gray kitten falling off of a toilet lid" />

</figure>

The Scrambledy Bamble is a creative style of movement Churro applies in a variety of contexts. Whether moving vertically or horizontally, he often responds to obstacles with a chaotic combination of flailing and grabbing with his tiny razor claws. 

### Supermodel Pose

<figure class="wiki-thumb right small">

  <img src="/assets/images/Snapchat-2144500326.jpg" alt="Churro, a small gray kitten, posing theatrically while being held by a dark haired woman, leaning to the side and staring down the camera lens" />

  <figcaption>Supermodel Pose</figcaption>

Churro is a star and he knows it. He likes to show off his style and sophistication by posing dramatically for the camera.

### Flop 'N' Plop

Churro will walk directly in front of someone's feet and plop right on down to lounge on the floor.
