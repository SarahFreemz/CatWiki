**Horchata** is one of the [Cinnaboys](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md), a pair of twin gray tuxedo kittens who recently joined the Fremont household. He is noted for his unique facial markings due to an incident at the [Kitten Factory](/places/kitten-factory.md), detailed [below](#the-paint-dip).

## Biography

Horchata was found outside with his feline Mama, his twin brother [Churro](/cats/churro.md), and two other kittens. [Dad](/people/jon.md)'s coworker brought them in and fostered them until the [Twins](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) were old enough to move into the Fremont household. While Horchata displayed some early signs of Shoulder Cat tendencies, he has proven much too skittish to perch on a shoulder. He has demonstrated a strong acuity for Biscuit Making, joining [Simon](/cats/simon.md) (the High Preist of Biscuits) as his Biscuit Acolyte.

### The Paint Dip
While walking through the paint tray at the [Kitten Factory](/places/kitten-factory.md), the department where their white socks were applied, Horchata tripped and dipped his nose into the paint a little bit. 

### The Ceiling Fan

From the second he caught sight of the Ceiling Fan's movement, Horchata has had A Mighty Grudge(tm). 

## Appearance & Style

Horchata's coat is a subtly variegated gray. He has perfect little ankle socks in the front (his business socks) and fluffy crew socks in the back (his party socks) to match his [brother](/cats/churro.md). His white chest looks like he's wearing a dress shirt under a little jacket. 

The main distinguishing feature between the [Twins](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) is the white Paint Splotch on Horchata's face, splitting his nose between black and pink. Researchers wonder if this mark may signify something about his destiny, though further study is needed to unravel its meaning. Horchata's Little Feets sport mostly pink Toe Beans with gray paw pads. However, two paws each boast a single gray Bean. It is unclear if these Beans bear meaning as well.

## Anatomy

Horchata has Big Ole Bug Eyes, leading to his nickname of the Bug-Eyed Bug Guy. His physique is bonier and more slender than his brother.

### Tail Chasing

Horchata is frequently distracted by his own tail (his [brother's](/cats/churro.md) too) and will chase it in little circles called the Spins. Researchers believe that this behavior is practice for hunting snakes.

### Fighting God

Horchata has acquired a grudge with the ceiling fan, for unknown reasons. He will stand up fully on his back paws and wave his hands at the ceiling fan when he catches sight of its movement. Additionally, he has been observed trying to fight a sunbeam from the window. Researchers and their colleagues have pondered the function of this behavior, theorizing that Horchata is training to fight God.
