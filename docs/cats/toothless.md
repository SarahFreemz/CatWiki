**Toothless Wilhelm Danger Fremont**, more commonly known as The Bub, was the Loveliest Boy in the Land and was the first creature to join the Fremont household. He was deeply loved and his presence is greatly missed.

## Biography

Toothless introduced himself to the Fremonts by dashing into a friends apartment, demanding food, love, and a home. Unable to keep him, the friend called [Sarah](/people/sarah.md) in a panic, and the Fremonts brought him home that night. 

Toothless engaged in a number of hobbies, such as Bird Watching, Grumping, and Tumbling on his window shelf. Whether keeping an eye out for birds, squirrels, delivery people, or Neighbor Cats, he spent many of his waking hours Watching. As Head of Security, he maintained a strict routine of checking every window to protect his home from Predators. One notable incident was the appearance of the Mirror Cat on the patio. Toothless responded by puffing himself up (particularly his tail) to intimidate the intruder through the glass door. He responded similarly to the appearance of a stray named Agatha. Other Neighbor Cats have been spotted over the years, but due to the Bub's tireless dedication to his role as Protector, none have ever gotten into the house. 

Toothless loved to sit in Mom's Lap, curling up for a nap and tucking his head into her knee. Sometimes he would do a dramatic Head Dive to really get comfortable. Given his enjoyment of Lap Time, he deeply despised Video Games and the Laptop for their encroachment on his seat of choice. Mom gratefully held him in her Lap for his last moments. 

## Appearance & Style

Toothless sported a sleek and glossy black coat with a patch of sparse white hairs on his chest. At various times he would sprout a single white whisker or eyebrow hair. Other individual white hairs occasionally grew randomly around his body, designated Dead Pixels. His eyes were a brilliant yellow,  fading to a pale green at the edges in certain light.

Toothless did not often wear a collar, preferring to be Nakey, but when he did dress up, he wore a black collar featuring skulls and crossbones.

## Anatomy

Toothless's directional hearing was objectively atrocious. When confronted with a loud noise (for example, a hair dryer), he would respond by smacking any object within reach, as though blaming it for making the sound.

The Bub was renowned for his particularly loud Purr Motor, expressing a variety of emotions with vigorous rumbling.

## Signature Moves or Behaviors
<div class="hatnote">For a full list of moves/poses, see <a href="/#/additional-observations/moves-and-poses">Moves and Poses</a>.</div>

### The Perfect Loaf

Toothless was truly a virtuoso of the art of the [Loaf](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=loaf), delicately tucking away all four paws and wrapping his tail to near invisibility. He also mastered the related [Sphinx pose](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=sphinx), often elevating it by elegantly crossing his paws in front.

### [Tumbles](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=tumbles)

Toothless frequently tumbled all about on his window shelf, rolling on his back and flip-flopping around. Such silly behavior could be escalated by pestering him from the underside of the shelf.
