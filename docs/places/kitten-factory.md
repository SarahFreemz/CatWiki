# The Kitten Factory

The **Kitten Factory** is where the [Cinnaboys](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) were assembled. Their white socks were applied in the paint department. While walking through the paint tray, [Horchata](/cats/horchata.md) tripped and dipped his nose in the paint a little bit, resulting in his distinctive facial markings.

It is likely that there was a mix-up of some parts while the boys were being assembled. Churro seems to have had [Big Ole Bat Ears](/cats/churro.md/#anatomy) installed in place of the standard kitten ears. Additionally, Horchata's eyes were mistakenly replaced with eyes a couple sizes too big, leading to the appearance of [Big Ole Bug Eyes](/cats/horchata.md/#anatomy).

Overall, the Kitten Factory may have shown some major oversights in terms of quality control, but the Fremonts would have the boys no other way.
