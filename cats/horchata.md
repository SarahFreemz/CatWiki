**Horchata** is one of the [Cinnaboys](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md), a pair of twin gray tuxedo kittens who recently joined the Fremont household. He is noted for his unique facial markings due to an incident at the [Kitten Factory](/places/kitten-factory.md), detailed [below](#the-paint-dip).

## Biography

Horchata was found outside with his feline Mama, his twin brother [Churro](/cats/churro.md), and two other kittens. [Dad](/people/jon.md)'s coworker brought them in and fostered them until the [Twins](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) were old enough to move into the Fremont household. Horchata has displayed some early signs of Shoulder Cat tendencies

### The Paint Dip
While walking through the paint tray at the [Kitten Factory](/places/kitten-factory.md), the department where their white socks were applied, Horchata tripped and dipped his nose into the paint a little bit. 

## Appearance & Style

Horchata's coat is a subtly variegated gray. He has perfect little ankle socks in the front (his business socks) and fluffy crew socks in the back (his party socks) to match his [brother](/cats/churro.md). His white chest looks like he's wearing a white shirt under a little jacket. The main distinguishing feature between the [Twins](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) is the white Paint Splotch on Horchata's face, splitting his nose between black and pink. Researchers wonder if this mark may signify something about his destiny, though further study is needed to unravel its meaning. Horchata's Little Feets sport mostly pink Toe Beans with gray paw pads. However, two paws boast a single gray Bean. It is unclear if these Beans are an omen as well.

Horchata wears a green collar day-to-day to distinguish him from his brother from behind, but he has a black bowtie with skulls and crossbones for formal events.
