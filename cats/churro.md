**Churro** is one of the [Cinnaboys](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md), a pair of twin gray tuxedo kittens who recently joined the Fremont household.

## Biography

Churro was found outside with his feline Mama, his twin brother [Horchata](/cats/horchata.md), and two other kittens. [Dad](/people/jon.md)'s coworker brought them in and fostered them until the [Twins](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md) were old enough to move into the Fremont household. He has quickly taken to snuggling [Mom](/people/sarah.md), his preferred spot being as high up on her collarbones as he can get.

## Appearance & Style

Churro is a beautiful solid gray color, except for his feet and chest, which are white. He has perfect little ankle socks in the front (his business socks) and fluffy crew socks in the back (his party socks) to match his [brother](/cats/horchata.md). His white chest looks like he's wearing a white shirt under a little jacket. He has a black nose.

Churro wears a blue collar to h him from his brother from behind. He has a dapper blue bowtie to wear on more formal occasions.
