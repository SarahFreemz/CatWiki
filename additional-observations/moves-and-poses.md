# Moves and Poses

A list of various moves, poses, positions, and behaviors that have been observed and identified within the Fremont household

## Biscuit Making

The making of biscuits is ubiquitous among the feline members of the Fremont household. Biscuit Making denotes the kneading of soft objects, such as pillows, blankets, or [Mom](/people/sarah.md). While the primary technique involves only the front paws, more advanced techniques may incorporate one or both of the rear feet.

Frequently observed subjects: [Simon](/cats/simon.md)

## Caterpillar/Worm

<figure class="wiki-thumb right small">
	<img src="/assets/images/IMG_20260309_010007153_HDR.jpg" alt="Simon, a long-haired brown tabby, demonstrating the Caterpillar pose on a gray couch" />
	<figcaption>Simon demonstrating the Caterpillar pose</figcaption>
</figure>

The Caterpillar/Worm pose is an elongated loaf position in which the subject stretches as long as possible, while remaining fairly narrow. This is differentiated from the [Sphinx](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=sphinx) pose by the positioning of the front feet. In this technique, they should be at least partially tucked away rather than regally stretched ahead. The back feet may trail behind for added length. Flatness is valued in this position, separating the Caterpillar from a traditional Loaf, along with the resting of the head rather than holding it aloft. The Worm variation can involve rolling around to reveal the subjects belly while keeping the feet tucked close.

Frequently observed subjects: [Simon](/cats/simon.md)

## Cinnamon Roll/Shrimp

Feline subjects will sometimes curl up into a little Cinnamon Roll (alternatively, Cinminroll) to conserve space while napping. Perfect roundness is a highly valued attribute, except in the case of the Shrimp variation which involves straightening out the back legs.

## Hangy Hands

	<img src="/assets/images/Snapchat-241371368.jpg" alt="Simon, a long-haired brown tabby cat, demonstrating the Hangy Hands posture" />
	<figcaption>Simon displaying his Hangy Hands</figcaption>

Hangy Hands is an elegant position, featuring paws delicately draped over the edge of whatever surface the subject is perching on. Advanced practitioners often incorporate the [Little Squints](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=little-squints) once they are comfortable with the technique (or are particularly sleepy). The Crossed Paws variation has also been observed.

Frequently observed subjects: [Simon](/cats/simon.md)

## Little Squints

Little Squints are a less pointed variation of the [Slow Blink](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=slow-blink). This behavior indicates a deep level of comfort, safety, and trust.

## Loaf

The Loaf is a staple in any feline household. The subject rests with their paws tucked away, resembling a fuzzy loaf of bread. A perfect loaf will have the tail wrapped around or even tucked.

## Long Arm of the Paw

Fremont felines have often been observed stretching one or both arms as far ahead as they can reach while resting. This has been labeled Long Arms, or the Long Arm of the Paw. Subjects have reached astonishing lengths, leading to calls for further study into the anatomical implications of such extension.

## Rasslin'

Rasslin' is a raucous activity comprised of tackling, pouncing, and tumbling between feline subjects. It very audible and is considered Upstairs Neighbor Behavior.

Frequently observed subjects: [The Cinnaboys](/additional-observations/cinnaboys-twins.md)

## Scrambledy Bamble

Very chaotic subjects may demonstrate a particularly exciting move known as the Scrambledy Bamble. This behavior is most often observed when the subject is attempting to leap onto a surface, climb an object/person, or catch themself mid-fall. It involves a creative combination of flailing, thrashing, grabbing, and clambering while navigating tricky terrain, often [Mahm](/people/sarah.md).

Frequently observed subjects: [Churro](/cats/churro.md)


## Slow Blink

The Slow Blink is without a doubt the highest praise one can receive from any feline. It indicates a deep level of trust on the part of the the Blinker toward the recipient. When this behavior is not aimed at anyone in particular, it may be referred to as a [Little Squint](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=little-squints).

Observed in all feline subjects

## Sphinx

The Sphinx may be the most regal and stately pose observers have documented. Much like a traditional Loaf, the subject may be seen in a vertical resting position with the back feet primly tucked away. The front paws, however, are proudly stretched forward, displaying a sense of relaxed nobility. Often paired with [Little Squints](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=little-squints). Another sophisticated variation on this pose 

Frequently observed subjects: [Simon](/cats/simon.md)

## Tumbles

Tumbling about on a window shelf in the sunshine is a favorite pastime of many Fremont felines. Subjects may roll around and flail their little paws. The observer can instigate or escalate the Tumbles by pestering the subject and scratching the underside of the shelf, though this action should be performed with caution.
