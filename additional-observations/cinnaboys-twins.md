The **Cinnaboys** are a pair of adorable gray kittens, [Churro](/cats/churro.md) and [Horchata](/cats/horchata.md), also known as Chooch and Chach. 

## Background

The Twins like to do everything together. This includes activities like eating, drinking, [Rasslin'](/additional-observations/moves-and-poses.md?id=rasslin), and even using the litterbox. This has resulted in statements from [Mom](/people/sarah.md) like "Please don't grab your brother's tail while he's pooping." They are nearly identical, except for the white patch on Horchata's face ()
